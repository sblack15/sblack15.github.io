A short index of research projects from my Ph.D. work. Each link goes to a project page with the paper, code, and details.

### [Multi-view Classification Using Hybrid Fusion and Mutual Distillation](/projects/multi-view-classification/)
<img src="/research/images/mvh.png" alt="multi-view hybrid" width="700"/>

Repurposes off-the-shelf hybrid CNN-Transformer networks for multi-view classification with either structured or unstructured views, using a novel fusion scheme and mutual distillation. *WACV 2024.*

---

### [Visualizing Paired Image Similarity in Transformer Networks](/projects/transformer-visualization/)
<img src="/research/images/transformers_viz.png" alt="transformers visualization" width="700"/>

A method for producing interpretable visualizations that, given a pair of images encoded with a Transformer, show which regions contributed to their similarity. *WACV 2022.*

---

### [Evaluation of Inpainting and Augmentation for Censored Image Queries](/projects/inpainting-augmentation/)
<img src="/research/images/inpainting.png" alt="inpainting teaser" width="700"/>

An extensive evaluation of image inpainting and data augmentation methods for mitigating the impact of censoring on image classification and retrieval. *IJCV 2021 / WACV 2020.*
