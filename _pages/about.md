I am a Senior Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech, where I research and develop computer vision systems deployed across Walmart stores. My work has included object tracking for video anomaly detection and product recognition using both image and text cues. I particularly enjoy using frontier VLMs to generate annotations for training smaller, specialized models.

Prior to joining Walmart, I received my Ph.D. at Temple University in the [Computer & Information Sciences Department](https://cis.temple.edu), as a member of the [Vision, Imaging, and Data Analysis Research Lab](https://github.com/vidarlab) under the supervision of [Dr. Richard Souvenir](https://cis.temple.edu/~souvenir/). My graduate work focused on deep learning for large-scale, fine-grained image classification and retrieval, including contributions to [Traffickcam](https://traffickcam.com/about-us/), a platform that aids in identifying hotel rooms to combat human trafficking. This spanned optimizing multi-view search, improving the interpretability of search tools, and enabling robust handling of partially-censored queries.

For more, see my [research projects](/research/) and [publications](/publications/).
